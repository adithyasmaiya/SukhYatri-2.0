Our story · Since 2019

We started with one Sumo, six strangers & a dream of comfortable India

Sukh (comfort) + Yatri (traveller). Today 1.2 lakh yatris, 48 routes, one obsession — India, felt deeply, travelled softly.

Why we exist

India is breathtaking. Getting around it shouldn't take your breath away.

Our founders — a Ladakhi driver-turned-guide, a Kerala homestay host, and a Mumbai product designer — kept meeting travellers who loved India but dreaded its logistics. SukhYatri was born to fix the unsexy stuff: verified stays, rested drivers, honest timelines, doctors on call, and food your stomach trusts.
